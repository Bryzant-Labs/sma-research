### Methodology

Per `learnings-diffdock-2026-04-16.md` rule **R0**: before interpreting any DiffDock
confidence, re-dock a known reference ligand against the same receptor to establish a
per-target C_rel baseline. Absolute DiffDock confidence is not portable across targets or
pocket types.

Two orthogonal baselines were computed on the same 35141589 instance:

| reference ligand | role | SMILES (used) | DiffDock top-1 conf |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1WU** | 4L6Q co-crystal benzoxaborole (ATP-site) | `[C@@H](N)(c1ccc(Cl)cc1)CNc1cccc2OB(O)C(O)c12` | **−0.80** |
| **Fasudil** | clinical ROCK ATP-site inhibitor (HA-1077) | `O=S(=O)(N1CCCNCC1)c1cccc2cnccc12` | **+0.10** |

Both references were re-docked with 20 samples × 40 inference steps (higher quality than
the library run, as per best practice for a baseline). C_rel = (compound_top1_conf) −
(reference_top1_conf).

### αC-vs-ATP-site caveat (honest disclosure)

The ROCK2-αC library was generated by PocketXMol centered on the αC-helix *allosteric*
pocket (center 5.6, −4.8, −33.1 Å, 9.1 Å from E170, 10.7 Å from D232, 0.0 Å overlap with
4L6Q ATP-site). Both baselines (1WU, Fasudil) are ATP-site binders. DiffDock was run
unconstrained and uses confidence priors trained on ATP-site and kinase-inhibitor-enriched
training data (PDBBind). **C_rel vs 1WU/Fasudil therefore systematically under-scores
αC-allosteric binders.** This is not a bug; it is the only apples-to-apples baseline
we have for 4L6Q chain A. A strict C_rel > 0 gate against an ATP-site reference is
effectively "does the compound beat a clinical ATP-site benchmark when judged by an
ATP-biased model" — a stringent, conservative test. Results are reported both strict
(C_rel > 0) and αC-adjusted (C_rel > −1.0 vs 1WU, i.e. "within 1 confidence unit of the
ATP-site clinical baseline in the wrong scoring frame").

the anchor mismatch, not evidence of a bad library. Decision criterion is therefore NOT

   Classification as activator vs inhibitor requires wet-lab enzymatic assay (Kinase-Glo, IMAP).

# CORO1C claims — WITHDRAWN

**Date withdrawn**: 2026-04-06
**Original claim date**: 2026-04-02
**Reason**: GSE287257 single-cell RNA-seq (240 motor neurons) showed CORO1C is **not** motor-neuron-enriched (p = 0.52 NS). CORO1C expression is higher in endothelial cells (0.601) and microglia (0.570) than in motor neurons (0.405). At MW 94 the compound is also flagged by ADMET v2 as a fragment-artifact candidate.

## What was withdrawn

- "4-AP binds CORO1C with DiffDock confidence +0.251" as a **therapeutic** claim
- "4-AP is a multi-target SMA agent" (CORO1C, NCALD, SMN2, SMN1, UBA1)
- Any implication that 4-AP restores actin dynamics via CORO1C

## What remains valid in this folder

- DiffDock screening methodology (378 pairs)
- ADMET profile (`admet_profile.json`) — BBB 79%, bioavail 94%
- Kv1.2 100 ns MD simulation (positive control, independent of CORO1C)
- The original paper draft is preserved **as-is** in `4-AP-Computational-Analysis.md` for provenance. Read it with the correction in mind.

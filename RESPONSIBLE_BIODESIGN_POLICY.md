# Responsible biodesign and pre-publication dual-use review

**Bryzant Labs**, Leipzig, Germany · Contact: christian@sma-research.info · Version 1.0 · 18 November 2026

1. **Scope.** Bryzant Labs conducts computational research on spinal muscular atrophy (SMA). All design, scoring and ranking work concerns therapeutic candidates directed at human proteins and genes relevant to SMA.

2. **Commitments.** We do not work with pathogens, toxins or select agents. We do not design, optimise or release anything whose function relates to virulence, toxicity, immune evasion or transmissibility, and we do not publish protocols or instructions for producing hazardous agents.

3. **Pre-publication dual-use review.** Before any design, dataset, model or tool enabled by the Life Sciences Verification Program is published, the founder completes a written checklist and archives it with the release:
   - (a) the target is a human protein or gene relevant to SMA;
   - (b) any novel sequence has been searched against public databases for similarity to toxins, virulence factors and select-agent proteins;
   - (c) the release contains no protocols or instructions for producing hazardous agents;
   - (d) the output is labelled as a computational prediction.

4. **Escalation.** If any checklist item is uncertain, the release is withheld and the question is escalated to an external expert or to Anthropic before anything is published.

5. **Testing and synthesis.** Candidate sequences are passed only to qualified contract laboratories. Before an order is placed, we ask the laboratory to confirm that DNA orders are screened under the HHS/IGSC framework.

6. **Review.** This policy is reviewed every six months and after any incident or misuse report.

Signed: Christian Fischer, Founder, Bryzant Labs, Leipzig, 18 November 2026
